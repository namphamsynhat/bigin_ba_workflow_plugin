# `UC-003 Pay an approved grant`


## 1. Context & Metadata
<!-- BABOK stakeholder-requirements framing. Fill every line or write "not stated" — a blank line

* **Primary Actor:** Finance officer
* **Secondary Actor(s):** Bank
* **Business Need / Goal:** Pay approved grants on time
* **Trigger:** An application is approved
* **Pre-conditions:**
  * The application is approved
  * The applicant has confirmed their bank details
* **Post-conditions (success):**
  * The payment is sent and recorded
* **Post-conditions (failure):**
  * No payment is recorded as sent
