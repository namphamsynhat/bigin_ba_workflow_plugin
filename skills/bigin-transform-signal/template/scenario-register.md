# Business Scenario Register

One row per cross-feature business scenario — a flow whose steps span more than one feature's FR
(a single-feature flow lives inside that feature's own FR, not here). Each participating feature's
hub mirrors a one-line pointer into its own `## Business Scenarios` section
(`- SCN-### <name> (step N of M)`); this file is the only place the full step sequence is written
out (conventions.md § Business Scenarios).

| SCN-### | Name | Steps (feature: what happens) | Status | Notes |
